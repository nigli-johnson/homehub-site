HomeHub

Privacy policy

Last updated November 19, 2026.

HomeHub is a private family dashboard that runs on one device in one home. This page explains what it does with your Google account when you sign in to show your calendars.

What HomeHub reads

  • Your calendars and events, read-only (Google's "See and download any calendar you can access" permission), so they can be shown on the hub.
  • Your email address, only to show which account is signed in.

HomeHub never creates, changes or deletes calendar events, and it doesn't read your email, contacts, files or anything else.

Where it's kept

  • Your sign-in and a copy of upcoming events are stored only on the HomeHub device in the home, to show them and to keep working if the internet drops.
  • Nothing is uploaded to any other server, and there are no ads, analytics or tracking.

Sharing

Your Google data is never sold, shared or given to anyone. It is shown only on the hub's screen and to devices in the home that the owner has approved.

HomeHub's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

  • On the hub: Settings > Calendars, then Remove next to your account. This deletes your sign-in from the hub, and your events stop showing.
  • From Google: open your Google account's third-party access and remove HomeHub.

Questions

Ask the hub's owner, or use the support email shown on Google's sign-in screen for HomeHub.
