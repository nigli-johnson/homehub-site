HomeHub

A family dashboard for the wall

HomeHub is a private, home-made touch screen that hangs on the wall of one family's home. It shows the family's calendars, to-do and grocery lists, weather and music in one place.

What it does with Google

  • Family members can sign in with their Google account, on the hub's own screen, so their calendars appear on it.
  • HomeHub shows calendars, and family members can add, edit or delete events from the hub's screen. It never changes anything on its own.
  • Everything stays on the hub, inside the home. Nothing is sent to anyone else.

Who can use it

HomeHub isn't a public service. It runs on a single device in one home, and only the people living there use it.
